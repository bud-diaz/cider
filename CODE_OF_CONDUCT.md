# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Cider to be a good experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that make this project open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive and healthy.

## Our standards

Behaviour that makes this community work:

- treating people with empathy and respect;
- taking criticism of your work seriously without taking it personally;
- giving criticism of someone else's work about the work;
- being willing to say "I was wrong about that";
- assuming the other person is trying to help until you have real evidence
  otherwise;
- helping newcomers, who will not know what you know.

Behaviour that does not:

- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks;
- harassment, public or private;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- sustained disruption of discussions;
- anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Technical disagreement

Cider is a technical project and people will disagree about technical decisions.
That is expected and useful.

Disagree about the decision. Bring the argument — the constraint, the benchmark,
the failure case. "This is wrong because X breaks under Y" is a contribution.
"Whoever wrote this doesn't understand Z" is not, and it is also usually wrong.

When a decision is settled, it is recorded in an [ADR](docs/adr/README.md) with
the alternatives that were considered and why they lost. Reopening a decision is
legitimate — bring new information, and write the superseding ADR.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions and chat — and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers.

> **Maintainer contact has not been published yet.** Cider is pre-alpha and has
> no public contribution channel; the address goes here before the repository
> opens for contributions. Until then, raise concerns directly with the project
> owner.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Reporters'
privacy and security will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
consequences determined by the project's leadership.

### Consequences

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning explaining what was inappropriate
   and why, possibly with a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and a
   period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — a period with no public or private interaction with the
   community.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from any public interaction within the
   project.
